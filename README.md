boAt Sales, Customer & Product Analytics Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard project built to explore sales performance, customer behaviour, product performance, and product returns for boAt.
Dashboard Preview
The PDF report contains three dashboard pages:
1. Overview — headline KPIs, top products by revenue, customer distribution by state, age-group split, product category mix, monthly revenue trend, and returns by category.
2. Customer Analytics — customer metrics, category/month/channel breakdowns, refund status, age groups, and slicers for payment method, gender, and date range.
3. Product Analytics — product and quantity KPIs, returns by month/category/reason, revenue by product, and filters for colour, review, and refund status.
